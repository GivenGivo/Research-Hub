About ZedAMR Research Hub

Antimicrobial Resistance Intelligence for Zambia

Our Mission

To provide timely, accurate, and actionable antimicrobial resistance (AMR) intelligence to healthcare professionals, policymakers, and researchers in Zambia. We aim to combat the growing threat of drug-resistant infections through evidence-based research and data-driven decision making.

What We Do

Research Summaries

Concise, digestible summaries of latest AMR research papers and findings relevant to Zambia.

Critical Alerts

Real-time alerts on emerging resistance patterns, outbreaks, and treatment failures.

Data & Statistics

Up-to-date statistics on resistance rates, costs, and trends across Zambian regions.

Expert Network

Connect with leading AMR specialists and researchers in Zambia and beyond.
